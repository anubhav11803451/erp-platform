## Active persistence and shared-contract inventory

The active Prisma schema declares **16 models**: the eight existing identity/onboarding models plus `InstitutionStaffMembership`, `InstitutionStaffInvitation`, `InstitutionStaffInvitationPendingGroup`, `InstitutionStaffGroupAssignment`, `InstitutionGroup`, `InstitutionStudent`, `InstitutionStudentGroupMembership`, and `InstitutionAttendanceMark` ([schema](../../packages/db/prisma/schema.prisma#L45-L321)). Its **5 enums** include the existing Account/Admin-invitation enums plus Staff invitation status, group type, and attendance mark status ([enum declarations](../../packages/db/prisma/schema.prisma#L11-L43)). There are **33 explicit `@relation(...)` annotations**. The new models use Institution ownership and composite keys/foreign keys to constrain related records to the same Institution. The migration adds the PostgreSQL partial/expression indexes required for lifecycle uniqueness; it is present in source but unapplied and not database-tested. The implemented relation shape is:

- `Account` has an optional `PlatformOwnerGrant`, an optional `InstitutionAdminMembership`, and many `Session` rows. It may also be referenced by invitation accepted/claimed fields and by optional invitation continuations.
- Each `PlatformOwnerGrant` belongs to one `Account`; a grant may be recorded as decision authority for multiple invitations.
- Each `Institution` belongs to one `LegalEntity`; a `LegalEntity` has many Institutions. An Institution has many Admin memberships and invitations.
- Each `InstitutionAdminMembership` links one Account to one Institution. `account_id` is its primary key, while Institution membership is indexed and not unique by `institution_id`.
- Each `InstitutionAdminInvitation` belongs to one Institution, can optionally reference accepted/claimed Accounts and a deciding Owner grant, and has many continuations. A continuation belongs to one invitation and can optionally reference an Account.
- Each Staff membership belongs to one Account and one Institution; it may have invitation history and group assignments. Staff invitations belong to one Institution, retain pending group selections, and reference the membership that accepts them. Each group, student, student/group membership, and attendance mark carries the same Institution owner; composite foreign keys constrain assignments, joins, invitations, and attendance to same-Institution records.
