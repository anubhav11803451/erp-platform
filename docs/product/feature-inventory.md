## Active persistence and shared-contract inventory

The active Prisma schema declares these **8 models**: `Account`, `PlatformOwnerGrant`, `Session`, `LegalEntity`, `Institution`, `InstitutionAdminMembership`, `InstitutionAdminInvitation`, and `InstitutionAdminInvitationContinuation` ([schema](../../packages/db/prisma/schema.prisma#L27-L156)). Its **2 enums** are `AccountIdentityKind` (`UNASSIGNED`, `PLATFORM_OWNER`, `INSTITUTION_ADMIN`) and `InstitutionAdminInvitationStatus` (`PENDING`, `PENDING_APPROVAL`, `APPROVED`, `REJECTED`, `REVOKED`, `EXPIRED`, `LEGACY_ACCEPTED`) ([enum declarations](../../packages/db/prisma/schema.prisma#L11-L25)). There are **14 explicit `@relation(...)` annotations**. The implemented relation shape is:

- `Account` has an optional `PlatformOwnerGrant`, an optional `InstitutionAdminMembership`, and many `Session` rows. It may also be referenced by invitation accepted/claimed fields and by optional invitation continuations.
- Each `PlatformOwnerGrant` belongs to one `Account`; a grant may be recorded as decision authority for multiple invitations.
- Each `Institution` belongs to one `LegalEntity`; a `LegalEntity` has many Institutions. An Institution has many Admin memberships and invitations.
- Each `InstitutionAdminMembership` links one Account to one Institution. `account_id` is its primary key, while Institution membership is indexed and not unique by `institution_id`.
- Each `InstitutionAdminInvitation` belongs to one Institution, can optionally reference accepted/claimed Accounts and a deciding Owner grant, and has many continuations. A continuation belongs to one invitation and can optionally reference an Account.
