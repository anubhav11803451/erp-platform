## 1. Product discovery

- **Stage status:** complete — user-approved scope and priority. The shared-schema architecture/data design and revised implementation plan were subsequently approved on 2026-11-02.
- **Target users / jobs-to-be-done:**
  - **Institution Admin:** understand which Institution is assigned to their account; set up groups; invite, assign, and revoke Staff access; oversee Institution operations.
  - **Institution Staff:** view and update the student roster and attendance only for groups assigned to them.
  - **Tutor:** an assignment associated with a group, not a separate access/permission role in this slice.
  - The repository describes tuition/coaching providers and K–12 institutions as audiences, but this feature's initial segment and geography are not validated.
- **Problem and evidence:** The user selected this capability and the current source inventory documents that the Institution Admin page is only a basic account/Institution overview, Institution-scoped operational APIs/data are not present, and additional staff/tutor management is absent from active source. This is an implementation gap against the user-approved feature scope, not evidence of customer demand or validated workflow pain. [`india-initial-user-problem-discovery.md`](india-initial-user-problem-discovery.md) is desk research, not customer evidence.
- **Current product behavior and source links (repository evidence, not runtime proof):**
  - [`feature-inventory.md`](../feature-inventory.md) records Institution Admin overview as `partial`, operations/data isolation as `partial`, and additional Staff/tutor management as `absent from repository`.
  - Active [`/institution` page](<../../../apps/frontend/src/pages/(app)/institution/page.tsx>) displays the signed-in Admin and assigned Institution, with no operational metrics or workflows.
  - Active [`schema.prisma`](../../../packages/db/prisma/schema.prisma) has `Account`, `InstitutionAdminMembership`, and onboarding/session models; it has no active operational Student, Staff membership, group, or attendance models. `Account.identity_kind` currently models `UNASSIGNED`, `PLATFORM_OWNER`, and `INSTITUTION_ADMIN`; the proposed Staff access type is not an implemented source role.
  - [`InstitutionAdminJwtAuthGuard`](../../../apps/backend/src/common/guards/institution-admin-jwt-auth.guard.ts) validates current Admin membership and attaches server-derived Institution scope. The current active business controllers do not consume it for operational records. No operational routes or active shared contracts exist.
  - [`system-overview.md`](../../architecture/system-overview.md), [`api-catalog.md`](../../architecture/api-catalog.md), and [`data-model.md`](../../architecture/data-model.md) document the active boundary as centralized PostgreSQL schema, four active controllers / 18 handlers, and eight active identity/onboarding models. These documents describe repository source, not a deployed system.
- **Options brainstormed:** User selected a focused Institution operations slice. Scope alternatives such as adding finance, family access, email invitation delivery, broad role matrices, or Platform Owner operational access are excluded from this record and require separate approval to reopen.
- **Ranked recommendation and trade-offs:** Proceed with the user's selected, narrow first operational slice: an honest Admin overview; groups; student roster; attendance; and one Institution-scoped Staff access type assignable only to groups. This prioritizes usable operational work while leaving future modules and role taxonomies separate. It does not resolve persistence topology, data-model design, or the older architectural decision noted below.
- **Approved in-scope:**
  1. A simple Admin `/institution` overview centered on the Admin account and assigned Institution; do not add fabricated metrics.
  2. First operational slice: student roster, school class/section or coaching batch groups, and attendance.
  3. One Institution-scoped Staff access type. Staff may view/update roster and attendance only for assigned groups. Admin manages the team/settings and sees Institution operations.
  4. Admin-managed Staff access through a manually shared one-time invitation link. An existing account uses its current password; a new user establishes their own password. No Staff access exists before invitation acceptance. Access can be revoked, and revocation must block subsequent protected API/session use.
  5. Tutor is a group assignment, not a distinct permission role.
  6. Institution is the tenant boundary; LegalEntity is grouping metadata. Backend authorization is authoritative.
  7. Preserve the existing theme and local shadcn/ui patterns.
- **Out of scope:** email delivery; fees/finance; family portal; other operational modules; separate Staff/Tutor permission matrices; Platform Owner access to operational records; fake metrics; visual theme changes; and any work outside the approved implementation plan in Section 4.
- **Functional outcomes / acceptance criteria (scope level; UI acceptance is completed in Section 2):**
  1. An Admin can identify their account and assigned Institution without seeing invented operational counts.
  2. An Admin can manage the team and settings, create/maintain Institution groups, and review Institution operations within the approved slice.
  3. An Admin can invite a Staff member using a manually shared one-time link, assign groups, and revoke access.
  4. A Staff member can view/update roster and attendance only within assigned groups. A Tutor assignment does not elevate or change the single Staff permission type.
  5. Invitation acceptance is required before Staff access; revocation blocks later protected API/session use. Backend authorization remains authoritative; frontend visibility is not a security control.
  6. No finance, family-portal, unrelated operational modules, distinct role matrices, or Platform Owner operational access is implied.

## 2. UI/UX design

- **Stage status:** Approved by the user on 2026-11-02 (“let approve this”). Subsequent architecture/data and implementation-plan approvals are recorded in Sections 3 and 4.
- **Applicability:** User-facing change.
- **Target personas and primary tasks:**
  - **Institution Admin:** identify the account/Institution context; set up groups; maintain the student roster and attendance; invite, assign, and revoke the single Staff access type; manage only settings selected for this slice.
  - **Institution Staff:** work with roster and attendance only for assigned groups.
  - **Tutor:** may be shown as an assignment/context label if selected, but is not a role, permission tier, or navigation category.
- **User flow and navigation/information architecture (recommendation; proposed paths are not existing routes):**
  - Keep the existing authenticated shell and `/institution` as the Admin landing route. Proposed Admin navigation: **Overview** (`/institution`), **Groups** (`/institution/groups`), **Students** (`/institution/students`), **Attendance** (`/institution/attendance`), **Team** (`/institution/team`), and **Settings** (`/institution/settings`). Team holds pending/accepted/revoked Staff invitations and group assignments; it offers Invite Staff and a per-person revoke action.
  - Proposed Staff navigation is limited to **My groups**, **Students**, and **Attendance**; Staff can select only assigned groups. A Staff-specific landing route is proposed but is not implemented or decided. Do not expose Team, Institution settings, unrelated modules, or a Tutor role.
  - Admin overview -> choose a task link; Groups -> create/select a group; Students -> select a group/filter, add or update roster entries; Attendance -> select group and date, mark/update the group's roster, explicitly save, review saved status.
  - Admin Team -> Invite Staff -> enter the selected invitee identifier -> assign one or more groups -> create invitation -> copy and manually share its one-time link. Invitee opens the proposed, distinct Staff acceptance route `/invite/staff`; show the Institution and access scope, then use the existing account's current password or establish a password for a new account. Only completed acceptance grants Staff access. The post-acceptance session and destination are open decisions; show a completion/next-step state only after that behavior is selected.
  - **Current routing boundary:** `useAuth.handleSignIn` sends Platform Owners to `/owner` and every other current identity to `/institution`; `AppLayout` redirects unauthenticated users to `/signin`; and `/institution` sends users without the current `INSTITUTION.ACCESS` permission to `/owner`. Current active identities do not include Staff. Therefore neither the current generic `/institution` sign-in destination nor its Admin-only fallback defines a safe Staff workspace or post-acceptance behavior. Options and a recommendation—not a decision—are recorded below.
  - Team -> Revoke -> review the named Staff member and assigned groups in a destructive confirmation -> confirm -> show revoked status and remove operational access. Explain that later protected use is denied; do not promise a forced logout or a particular session-invalidation mechanism from UI copy.
  - Settings is an Admin-only entry because settings management is in approved scope, but expose only fields explicitly selected after the open settings decisions below. Do not infer settings from LegalEntity membership or present LegalEntity as the tenant/workspace selector.
  - Keep first-Admin onboarding decisions and the active `/invite/admin` journey separate. `/invite/admin` renders the current first-Admin `InvitationAcceptance` claim flow, which requests Owner review and does not itself grant workspace access or establish a workspace session. The proposed Staff acceptance flow is not an extension of that flow: do not copy its API, claim/approval, or authority semantics. Reuse visual primitives only where appropriate.
